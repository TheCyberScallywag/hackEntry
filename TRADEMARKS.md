The organisation names *DataKind UK*, *Cyber Scallywags* and *InterLinkRCT* (Interlink RCT)
appear as illustrative, configurable demo labels (`ORG_NAMES`). Their logos, one photograph each
and a short summary of their public "about" pages are kept in `data/orgs/` to identify them on
the demo's showcase page; renaming an organisation with `ORG_NAMES` removes them from the app.
The projects, people and accounts shown beside them are invented. No endorsement or partnership
is implied; get each organisation's permission before deploying the demo publicly with their
marks, or delete `data/orgs/`. Relate is a product of its respective
