A skill counts as covered once someone who offers it is on the team. ★ prefers this project's cause. “Done this before” marks earlier work on the same kind of cause: this organisation's own record, and a summary of work elsewhere only where the volunteer has chosen to share it. Candidates are opted-in volunteers who have and offer the skill and have excluded neither the cause nor any required skill; the project lead chooses who to invite. Close lists volunteers who do not have the skill but offer a related one or one in the same skill area: they are not candidates and cannot be invited for it, but they may be worth a conversation.
